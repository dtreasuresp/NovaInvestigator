This Privacy Policy describes how DGTECNOVA S.R.L. ("DGTECNOVA", "we", "us"
or "our") processes personal data when a person visits a NovaResearch
property, creates an account, uses the NovaResearch platform, uses the
Research application, interacts with NovAi, contacts support or otherwise
interacts with DGTECNOVA.

This policy is not a Data Processing Agreement. For a business customer that
uses NovaResearch to process personal data on its behalf, the
`DATA_PROCESSING_AGREEMENT.md` applies to that processor activity.

## 3. Personal data we may process

The categories depend on the services used and the configuration selected.

### 3.1 Account and identity data

This may include name, surname, email address, telephone number, mobile
number, country, authentication identifiers, profile information, account
status, tenant membership, workspace membership, role and capability
assignments.

### 3.2 Billing and purchase data

This may include billing email, country, tax identifier, provider customer
identifier, subscription identifier, invoice identifier, invoice status,
payment status, currency, amounts required for reconciliation, hosted-invoice
URL, purchase name, purchase surname, mobile number and purchase address.

NovaResearch is not intended to collect or store full payment-card numbers,
card security codes or equivalent payment credentials. Payment credentials are
entered into the applicable payment provider's interface.

### 3.3 Security and service data

This may include IP address, user-agent information, session and cookie
identifiers, timestamps, correlation identifiers, audit events, login
information, device or browser information, error information, access events
and tenant or workspace references.

Excluding this information from the definition of Customer Data does not make
it anonymous. It may remain personal data under applicable law.

### 3.4 Research, project and user content

Depending on the tenant and feature, this may include documents, evidence,
comments, research records, factors, relationships, matrices, project records,
work packages, activities, tasks, budgets, user-generated text, prompts,
conversations, uploaded files, AI inputs and AI outputs.

Business customers are responsible for determining whether the content they
submit contains personal data and for providing the notices and legal bases
required for that content.

### 3.5 Communications and support

This may include contact details, support requests, correspondence, account
information, diagnostic details supplied by the user and records of
communications with DGTECNOVA.

### 3.6 Cookies, analytics and similar technologies

Where enabled, DGTECNOVA may use necessary cookies, session technologies,
security technologies, analytics and performance measurement. The applicable
cookie notice must identify each technology, purpose, provider, retention and
consent requirement before non-essential technologies are enabled.

## 4. Sources of personal data

We may receive personal data:

- from support and service integrations;

## 6. Allocation of responsibility

### 6.1 DGTECNOVA as an independent controller

DGTECNOVA determines the purposes of processing for account administration,
tenant and workspace administration, contractual relationship management,
support, service communications, security, abuse prevention, fraud prevention,
legal and tax compliance, audit, reliability, entitlement enforcement and
limited billing reconciliation.

### 6.2 Business customer as controller

A business customer determines the purposes and means of personal data it
submits about its employees, customers, suppliers, contacts, research
participants or other individuals. The business customer is responsible for
its privacy notices, legal bases, instructions, retention decisions and
responses to data-subject requests concerning that content.

DGTECNOVA acts as processor or equivalent service provider for that content
under the Data Processing Agreement.

### 6.3 Direct consumers

When an individual uses NovaResearch directly for personal purposes,
DGTECNOVA generally acts as controller for the account, service, support,
security and contract relationship. Consumer-specific terms and mandatory
consumer protections apply.

### 6.4 Stripe

Stripe provides and operates Checkout, Billing and Customer Portal services
used for payments and subscriptions. Stripe may act as an independent
controller for its own purposes, including payment processing, fraud
prevention, security, disputes and legal compliance. Depending on the Stripe
product, jurisdiction and applicable agreement, Stripe may act as a processor
or service provider for other activities.

Stripe's privacy notice, services agreement, data-processing terms and
product-specific terms govern Stripe's processing. DGTECNOVA does not control
Stripe's independent purposes or payment infrastructure.

NovaResearch may retain limited Stripe references needed for contractual
administration, access entitlements, support, audit, reconciliation and legal
compliance. Those references do not give DGTECNOVA control over full card data
or Stripe's hosted billing records.

## 8. Artificial intelligence

NovAi and other AI features may process prompts, documents, evidence,
conversation context, memory entries, tool results and generated outputs to
provide the selected feature.

Unless expressly agreed otherwise, DGTECNOVA will not use Customer Data or
Customer confidential information to train a general-purpose third-party AI
model for the benefit of third parties. Provider-specific retention and
training settings must be reviewed before a provider receives Customer Data.

AI outputs may be inaccurate. Users must apply human review and must not use
AI output as the sole basis for decisions with legal or similarly significant
effects on a person.

The `NOVAI_TERMS.md` document provides additional product-specific information.

other markets may impose additional requirements. This policy does not claim

## 11. Data-subject rights

Subject to applicable law, a person may have rights to access, correct, delete,
restrict, object to, receive or port personal data, withdraw consent and
challenge certain automated decisions.

Requests concerning:

- DGTECNOVA's independent-controller processing should be sent to the privacy
  contact stated above;
- Customer-controlled tenant content should normally be sent to the relevant
  business customer, with DGTECNOVA providing assistance under the DPA; and
- Stripe's independent processing should be directed to Stripe.

We may need to verify identity and authority before responding. We will not
disclose another person's data without a lawful basis.
