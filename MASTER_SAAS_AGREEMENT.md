## 1. Parties and purpose

This Agreement is between DGTECNOVA S.R.L. ("DGTECNOVA", "Provider", "we",
"us" or "our") and the business customer identified in an Order Form
("Customer"). The registered legal name, address, registration number, tax
identification and authorized representative of DGTECNOVA must be completed in
the Order Form before this Agreement is used.

For consumer transactions, the individual customer is governed by
`CONSUMER_TERMS.md`. Consumer rights that cannot legally be waived prevail over
this Agreement.

This Agreement governs authorized access to NovaResearch and the services,
applications, APIs, integrations, professional services and product-specific
features identified in the applicable Order Form.

## 2. Product identities and scope

### 2.1 NovaResearch

NovaResearch is DGTECNOVA's multi-tenant platform and suite. It may provide
tenant and workspace administration, user and role management, entitlement
controls, billing references, audit capabilities and related enterprise
services.

### 2.2 Research

Research is the application within NovaResearch for strategic research,
organizational diagnosis, evidence management, analytical workflows and
strategic methodologies such as DAFO/SWOT, EFI, EFE, CAME and QSPM.

### 2.3 Projects

Projects is the execution environment for projects, work packages, activities,
budgets, Kanban workflows, responsibilities, monitoring and
research-to-execution traceability.

### 2.4 NovAi

NovAi is an independent DGTECNOVA AI product. It is not a child module or
property of Research or NovaResearch. It may integrate with NovaResearch,
customer systems and third parties through authorized APIs, MCP interfaces,
SDKs, connectors and tools.

The service scope, enabled modules, usage limits and deployment model are
defined by the Order Form and applicable product terms. A feature described as
available may depend on configuration, plan, region, provider availability or
technical limits.

## 4. Definitions

### 4.1 Customer Data

Customer Data means content submitted by or for Customer to an authorized
tenant, including documents, evidence, research records, matrices, project
records, user-generated text, configurations and business information.

Customer Data does not include Provider IP or Service Data. Excluding data from
this definition does not remove any privacy obligation when the data identifies
an individual.

### 4.2 Provider IP

Provider IP means DGTECNOVA's original software expression, source code, object
code, architecture, interfaces, workflows, schemas, data models, analytical
engines, orchestration, integrations, documentation, know-how, confidential
information, trade secrets, security mechanisms, billing and entitlement
architecture and proprietary improvements.

Provider IP does not include Customer Data, third-party materials or general
ideas, facts, mathematical principles or publicly available methodologies.

### 4.3 Service Data

Service Data means technical, operational, security, audit, usage and
reconciliation information generated while providing the Services. Service Data
may include identifiers, IP addresses, user-agent information, timestamps,
correlation identifiers and tenant references. DGTECNOVA processes Service Data
under the Privacy Policy and applicable law.

### 4.4 Authorized User

An Authorized User is a person authorized by Customer to use the Services for
Customer's tenant. Customer is responsible for assigning access according to
its own authority and internal controls, subject to the security and
authorization controls implemented by DGTECNOVA.

### 4.5 Services

Services means the hosted NovaResearch services and any other service expressly
identified in an Order Form.

Customer decides what Customer Data to submit and, where applicable, determines

Customer must have a lawful basis and all notices, permissions and
authorizations required to submit personal data. Customer must not submit

### 7.2 DGTECNOVA's independent-controller processing

DGTECNOVA acts as an independent controller or equivalent responsible party for
purposes it determines itself, including:

- account and identity administration;
- tenant, workspace and membership administration;
- contractual relationship management;
- service security, abuse prevention and fraud prevention;
- support and service communications;
- legal, tax, audit and compliance obligations;
- platform telemetry and reliability;
- entitlement and access enforcement; and
- limited billing and payment references required to operate the Services.

The Privacy Policy describes these processing activities and their retention.

### 7.3 Processing instructions

DGTECNOVA may use aggregated or anonymized information that cannot reasonably
identify Customer or an individual for security, reliability, capacity
planning, service analytics and product improvement. Pseudonymized or
de-identified data that can reasonably be re-identified remains subject to
privacy obligations.

DGTECNOVA does not sell Customer's confidential business information.

## 8. Privacy, security and incidents

The Privacy Policy applies to DGTECNOVA's independent-controller processing.
The Data Processing Agreement applies to processor activities for business

DGTECNOVA will maintain a security program appropriate to the nature, scope and
risk of the Services. The program may include encryption in transit, encryption
at rest where supported by the relevant infrastructure, access controls,

practices.

Reference to NIST, ISO/IEC 27001 or ISO/IEC 42001 does not constitute a
certification claim. DGTECNOVA will identify an actual certification only when
it holds and maintains that certification.

DGTECNOVA will notify Customer of a confirmed security incident affecting
Customer Data without undue delay and in accordance with the time and content
requirements in the DPA. DGTECNOVA will not disclose information when doing so
would compromise an investigation or violate law, but will provide the
cooperation required by applicable law.

Customer remains responsible for credentials, Authorized Users, endpoints,
Customer configurations and its own legal obligations.

The Services may use cloud infrastructure, databases, authentication, email,

### 9.1 Stripe's role

Stripe provides and operates the payment, subscription-billing, Checkout and
Customer Portal infrastructure used by the Services. DGTECNOVA does not operate

Stripe may process personal data as an independent controller for its own
purposes, including payment processing, fraud prevention, security, disputes,

DGTECNOVA does not intentionally collect or store full payment-card numbers,
card security codes or equivalent payment credentials through NovaResearch.

and product terms govern Stripe's processing.

NovaResearch may retain limited references received from Stripe, including
provider identifiers, billing email, country, tax identifiers, invoice

Nothing in this Agreement designates Stripe as merchant of record, invoice
issuer or tax provider unless the applicable commercial agreement expressly
states so.

Customer remains responsible for human review and final decisions. Customer
