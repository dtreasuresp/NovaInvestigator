# NovAi Product Terms

**Status:** Draft - requires legal, technical and provider review before
publication
**Version:** 1.0
**Effective date:** [TO BE COMPLETED]

NovAi is an independent DGTECNOVA AI product. It may integrate with
NovaResearch, Research, customer systems and third-party systems through
authorized APIs, MCP interfaces, SDKs, connectors and tools. This integration
does not make NovAi a child module or property of NovaResearch or Research.

These terms supplement the Master SaaS Agreement. The Order Form must identify
which NovAi features, providers, retention settings and deployment model are
enabled.

## 1. AI-assisted functionality

NovAi may provide classification, retrieval, summarization, evidence
analysis, contradiction detection, methodological checks, recommendations,
generated text, project suggestions, tool orchestration and other outputs.

Features depend on configuration, plan, model availability, provider terms,
region, context limits and technical safeguards.

## 2. Inputs, context and outputs

Inputs may include prompts, documents, evidence, research records, project
records, conversation context, memory entries and tool results. Outputs may
include generated text, citations, classifications, calculations,
recommendations and structured results.

Outputs are probabilistic. They may be incorrect, incomplete, stale,
unsupported or unsuitable for a particular purpose. Users must verify
important claims and sources before relying on an output.

Customer retains its rights in Customer Data and receives the use rights
stated in the applicable agreement for outputs generated from that data,
subject to third-party rights, model limitations and applicable law.

## 3. Human oversight and restricted decisions

NovAi is a decision-support product. It does not replace professional, legal,
financial, medical, regulatory or management judgment.

Customer and users must maintain human review for material decisions. They
must not use NovAi as the sole basis for a decision that materially affects a
person's legal, employment, financial, health, housing, education or similar
rights where applicable law requires additional safeguards.

## 4. Data use and training

Unless the Order Form expressly says otherwise, DGTECNOVA will not use
Customer Data or Customer confidential information to train a general-purpose
third-party AI model for the benefit of third parties.

NovAi may process inputs and outputs as necessary to provide the selected
feature, enforce security, prevent abuse, maintain auditability, troubleshoot
failures and comply with law. The applicable Privacy Policy and DPA govern the
role and purpose for each processing activity.

Third-party model providers may have their own retention, safety and data-use
terms. A provider must be reviewed and listed before Customer Data is sent to
it for a paid or enterprise feature.

## 6. Model routing and provider changes

NovAi may route a request to a configured model or provider according to
availability, capability, cost, security, privacy and product policy. The
provider and processing location must be disclosed where required by law or
the applicable Order Form.

DGTECNOVA may replace a provider when reasonably necessary for security,
reliability, legal compliance, availability or product operation. A material
change to a contractual data-use commitment requires the notice or remedy
stated in the Order Form or DPA.

## 7. Tools and MCP

Tool and MCP access is limited by authorization, tenant scope, capability
checks, rate limits and the configuration of the relevant feature.

Users must review tool permissions and must not authorize a tool to access
data or perform an action beyond its documented purpose. NovAi does not grant
permission to bypass an external system's terms, authentication or security
controls.

External tools may be controlled by third parties. Their terms, availability,
security and privacy practices apply to their own processing.

## 8. Evidence and citations

Where a feature provides evidence, citations or source references, those
references support review but do not guarantee that an output is true,
complete, current or legally sufficient. Users remain responsible for
verifying sources and preserving the records required for their work.

## 9. Quotas and service limits

NovAi use may be subject to plan limits, daily or monthly quotas, token
budgets, context limits, rate limits and provider limits. The applicable
limits must be disclosed in the Order Form, product interface or service
documentation.

DGTECNOVA may reject, limit or defer a request when required to protect
security, privacy, capacity, a provider limit or a legal restriction.

## 10. Prohibited AI use

Users must not use NovAi to:

- generate malware, credentials or instructions for unauthorized intrusion;
- evade safeguards, access controls or legal obligations;
- unlawfully profile, discriminate against or surveil a person;
- expose another tenant's data;
- submit payment-card credentials or secrets;
- make an unreviewed high-impact decision where law requires human oversight;
- misrepresent generated content as verified human or official content; or
- infringe a third party's rights.

## 11. Third-party models and services

Third-party models, APIs, tools and connectors remain subject to their own
licenses, service limits and privacy terms. The current provider information
must be maintained in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` or the applicable provider
register.

DGTECNOVA is not responsible for an independent provider's processing,
availability, output or legal compliance, but remains responsible for its own
integration and contractual obligations.

## 12. No guarantee of output

NovAi does not guarantee that an output is accurate, unique, complete,
non-infringing, suitable for a particular decision or available without
interruption. Nothing in these terms excludes mandatory statutory rights or
liability that cannot lawfully be excluded.
