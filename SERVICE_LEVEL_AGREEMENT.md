## 2. Availability

Availability is measured for the covered service at the agreed service
boundary. Scheduled maintenance, emergency maintenance, customer
configuration, unsupported integrations, force majeure, third-party outages,
network failures outside DGTECNOVA's control and suspension permitted by the
Agreement are excluded only when stated in the Order Form.

No service-level target applies to a feature, region, provider or integration
that the Order Form does not identify.
