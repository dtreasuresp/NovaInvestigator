## 4. License obligations

Before releasing a version, DGTECNOVA must review whether any component
requires:

- attribution or preservation of copyright notices;
- inclusion of a license text;
- disclosure of source code or corresponding source;
- disclosure of modifications;
- distribution under a reciprocal license;
- patent notices or patent grants;
- notices in a user interface or documentation; or
- additional restrictions on commercial distribution.

No component license may be removed, narrowed or replaced by this document.
Where a third-party license grants a mandatory right, that right prevails over
the proprietary restrictions in `LICENSE.md`.
