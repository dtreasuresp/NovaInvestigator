# NovaResearch Acceptable Use Policy

**Status:** Draft - requires legal review before publication
**Version:** 1.0
**Effective date:** [TO BE COMPLETED]

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") applies to NovaResearch, Research, Projects,
NovAi, APIs, integrations and authorized MCP connections. It supplements the
Master SaaS Agreement and does not remove rights that cannot legally be
restricted.

## 1. Permitted use

Customers and Authorized Users may use the Services for lawful research,
organizational analysis, strategic planning, project execution, evidence
management and related business or personal purposes enabled by their plan.

Use must respect tenant boundaries, access controls, usage limits, third-party
rights, privacy rights and the instructions for each feature.

## 2. Prohibited conduct

No person may use the Services to:

1. violate applicable law, sanctions or export restrictions;
2. infringe copyright, privacy, confidentiality, trademark or other rights;
3. access another tenant, workspace, account or data without authorization;
4. bypass authentication, entitlements, rate limits, safety controls or
   tenant isolation;
5. probe, scan, overload, disrupt or impair the Services without written
   authorization;
6. upload malware, ransomware, credentials, payment-card data or harmful code;
7. impersonate another person or misrepresent authorization;
8. use the Services for unlawful surveillance, harassment, discrimination or
   exploitation;
9. process personal data without an appropriate legal basis or required notice;
10. use AI output as the sole basis for a legally or similarly significant
    decision about a person where applicable law requires safeguards;
11. use the Services to create or operate a competing product based on
    Provider IP; or
12. scrape, systematically extract or reproduce protected functionality.

Nothing in this list prohibits lawful interoperability, portability, security
research or another activity protected by mandatory law.

## 3. Customer content

Customer is responsible for the lawfulness, accuracy, permissions and
security of content it submits. Customer must not submit special-category,
children's, health, biometric, financial-account or other restricted data
unless the applicable service and agreement expressly support it.

Customer must not upload secrets, private keys, passwords or full payment-card
credentials. If such information is submitted accidentally, Customer must
notify DGTECNOVA and follow the incident procedure.

## 4. AI and NovAi

AI output may be incomplete, inaccurate or unsupported. Users must review
outputs before relying on them. Additional rules for prompts, memory, models,
tools, provider routing and data use are in `NOVAI_TERMS.md`.

Users must not use NovAi to make an automated decision that unlawfully affects
another person or to evade a regulatory, professional or human-review
requirement.

## 5. APIs and MCP connections

API and MCP access must use authorized credentials, documented endpoints and
published limits. Customers must protect credentials, identify automated
clients where required and avoid sending unnecessary personal data.

Unauthorized tools, connectors, prompt injection attempts, data exfiltration,
tool chaining designed to bypass controls and hidden-credential extraction are
prohibited.

## 6. Reporting

Suspected abuse, unauthorized access or security vulnerabilities must be
reported through the private security channel in `SECURITY.MD`. Reports must
not include unnecessary personal data, secrets or publicly exploitable
instructions.

## 7. Enforcement

DGTECNOVA may investigate suspected violations and may warn, limit, suspend
or terminate access to the extent reasonably necessary to protect users,
security, legal compliance or the Services. Where practicable, DGTECNOVA will
provide notice and an opportunity to cure.

Emergency action may occur without advance notice when delay creates a
material security, legal or operational risk. DGTECNOVA will limit the action
to the affected scope where reasonably possible.

## 8. Changes

Material changes to this AUP require the notice or consent required by the
Master SaaS Agreement and applicable law. The published version must state its
effective date.
